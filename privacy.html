Privacy

A plain-language explanation of what SafeSpace stores and who can see it.

What's stored

If you create an account: your email address and password (handled entirely by our authentication provider, Supabase — SafeSpace itself never sees or stores your password).

If you save a journal entry: its title, mood, content, and date.

Your theme preference (light/dark) and your most recent check-in mood are kept only in your browser's local storage — they're never sent anywhere and never leave your device.

Who can see your journal

Through the app, only you. Every journal entry is tied to your account, and the database enforces this with row-level security rules — another user, even one who knew your account existed, has no way to request or browse your entries through SafeSpace. As with any hosted database, the site's administrator retains underlying access to the raw data through the hosting provider's dashboard, the same as with any other web service — but nothing in the app's normal operation exposes one user's entries to another.

A note for @psd202.org accounts

Our email provider isn't able to deliver confirmation emails to psd202.org inboxes. To keep sign-up usable for that domain, accounts created with a psd202.org address are automatically marked confirmed without a confirmation email being sent. This means we don't verify that you actually control the psd202.org address you sign up with.

The same limitation applies to password resets: if you forget your password on a psd202.org account, the "reset your password" email won't arrive either. In that case, the account has to be reset manually by the site administrator rather than through the self-service link.

Deleting your data

You can delete any individual journal entry yourself from the Journal History page at any time. To have your account and all associated data removed entirely, contact the site administrator (see below).

Cookies & tracking

SafeSpace does not use tracking cookies or any third-party analytics. Local storage is used only for the functional preferences described above.

Contact

SafeSpace is a personal project made by Shaurya Gupta. For data questions or deletion requests, reach out directly.
